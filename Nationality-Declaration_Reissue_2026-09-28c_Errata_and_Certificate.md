## E-ND-12 — Venue-clarity conformance (one change class, two text loci; the allocation of record is untouched)

**The factual answer to the author's question, of record:** edition 2026-09-28b already named both party classes at the venue paragraph — "sitting for cases **between Moors** and for **mixed cases between Moors and United States citizens or their protected persons or protégés**" — Moor against Moor, and Moor against a United States citizen, protected person, or protégé. Two loci, however, carried the abbreviated form "for mixed cases" alone (the attestation and the edition-recital summary class), and no locus carried the concrete bidirectional party example that the Chapter 21 statement of record carries ("a Moorish subject against a guest, or a guest against a Moorish subject").

**The change (exactly four lines differ from edition 2026-09-28b — lines 4, 6, 34, 116; all other lines byte-identical):**

.1 **Edition identifier (line 4)** — Nationality-Declaration-TEMPLATE-HARMONIZED-2026-09-28c.

.2 **Edition recital (line 6)** — the E-ND-12 recital added, carrying forward E-ND-1 through E-ND-11 in full.

.3 **Venue paragraph (line 34, Section I).** The bidirectional party example of record is now stated in the instrument text itself: the Chapter 21 Domestic Tribunal sits "for cases between Moors and for mixed cases between Moors and United States citizens or their protected persons or protégés — **a Moorish subject against a citizen or protégé of the United States, or a citizen or protégé of the United States against a Moorish subject** — as the Empire's domestic venue of record". This conforms the instrument to the Chapter 21 statement of record; the allocation of record (the author's directive of 2026-09-26) is restated verbatim, not altered.

.4 **Attestation (line 116).** The abbreviated "for cases between Moors and for mixed cases" is expanded to the full party-class form: "for cases between Moors and for mixed cases between Moors and United States citizens or their protected persons or protégés — and not to any municipal venue the hiba displaces."

**Allocation of record (per the author's directive of 2026-09-26, preserved verbatim in the register), carried forward unchanged:** Article 20 — venue lies in the consular jurisdiction for cases involving the United States, its citizens, and their protected persons or protégés. Article 21 — venue lies in the domestic tribunal of the Empire of Morocco for cases between Moors and for mixed cases between Moors and United States citizens or their protected persons or protégés. This allocation governs every venue statement of record.
