## E-ND-11 — Venue-allocation conformance (one change class, three loci)

**What happened, disclosed plainly:** on 2026-09-26 the desk applied the author's venue-allocation directive to the tribunal site pages (instruments.html and tribunal.html) as page-level edits. On 2026-09-28 the site pages were regenerated from the declaration text of record, which did not then carry the allocation; the page-level fix was wiped in the regeneration, and the desk's verification compared transmission hashes, not content survival — the same verification gap class as the print-family defect of earlier today (E-ND-10 addendum), caught by the author's own reading. The author asked whether the removal was by mistake: it was — an unintended loss, never a deliberate deletion.

**The change (exactly three loci in the instrument text, all other lines byte-identical to edition 2026-09-28):**

.1 **Venue paragraph (Section I, "Scope of this declaration").** The party-class allocation of the venues of record is now stated in the instrument text itself: Article 20 — the consul decides between the parties *in cases involving the United States, its citizens, and their protected persons or protégés*; Article 21 — the Chapter 21 Domestic Tribunal sits *for cases between Moors and for mixed cases between Moors and United States citizens or their protected persons or protégés*.

.2 **Remedy machinery (Section IV).** "consular jurisdiction (Articles 20–21), and estoppel by admission" conformed to "the venues of the grant (Articles 20–21), and estoppel by admission."

.3 **Attestation.** "any dispute is subject to consular jurisdiction (Article 20) before the Chapter 21 Domestic Tribunal of record, not domestic venue" conformed to "any dispute is subject to the venue the grant itself establishes — consular jurisdiction (Article 20) for cases involving the United States, its citizens, and their protected persons or protégés, and the domestic tribunal of the Empire of Morocco (Article 21) for cases between Moors and for mixed cases — and not to any municipal venue the hiba displaces."

**Allocation of record (per the author's directive of 2026-09-26, preserved verbatim in the register):** Article 20 — venue lies in the consular jurisdiction for cases involving the United States, its citizens, and their protected persons or protégés. Article 21 — venue lies in the domestic tribunal of the Empire of Morocco for cases between Moors and for mixed cases between Moors and United States citizens or their protected persons or protégés. This allocation governs every venue statement of record.
