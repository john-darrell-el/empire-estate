Why there is no gate here

  1. Possession confers nothing. Execution by a true heir is what perfects the instrument — the text itself is inert in unentitled hands. Lock 3: perfection of status is not contingent on any third party’s act.
  2. The public record is the armor. A text published openly, with its edition identifier and SHA-256 hash, cannot be misrepresented as secret, sold as contraband, or altered without detection.
  3. No beneficiary registry is kept. This office does not build lists of heirs. Verification exists only inside paid certification services, where identity must be established for the certificate itself.
  4. The waqf is open by definition. An endowed library gates no one out of reading.
